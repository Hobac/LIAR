1. A first-order-logic JSON formula containing natural-language terms.

You need to try to resolve every natural language term!
If there was no suitable suggestion provided, replace the natural language term with <error>.
Constant values like numbers do not have to be resolved if they appear in a comparison.

TYPE: property | LABEL: occupation | ID: P106 | DESCRIPTION: occupation of a person

* Do not automatically choose the first suggestion.
